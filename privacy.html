Benji's Kitchen privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Benji's Kitchen helps you keep track of your food and cook what expires first. This page explains what happens to your information. The short version: there's no account, and your food, lists, and plans stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your food, recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, settings, badges, and hats are saved only on your device. Benji's Kitchen doesn't collect them, and we can't see them. Clearing the app's data or your browser data can erase them, so use Export a backup in Settings to keep a copy.

Shared kitchens

If you turn on a shared kitchen, your food, shopping lists, and meal plan are copied to the Benji's Kitchen sync server so the other phones in your home can get them. Before anything leaves your phone, it's locked (encrypted) with your kitchen code, and the code itself is never sent. The server only stores the locked copy, so we can't read it. A shared kitchen that nobody opens for 180 days is deleted from the server. Stop sharing anytime in Settings, under Share or move your kitchen.

Features that use outside services

  • Barcode scanning: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product name. Nothing else is sent.
  • Reading a date or a receipt from a photo: a free text reading tool is downloaded the first time you use it. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Fonts: the app's fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Kitchen links: if you share a kitchen link, it contains your kitchen data. Share it only with people you trust.

Counting visits

To see how many people use Benji's Kitchen, the website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts visits and which pages are opened, along with the kind of device, browser, and country. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across websites, and never sees your food, lists, or anything you type. We can't tell who you are from it.

Purchases

Everything that helps you save food and money is free. If you buy an optional extra from Benji's shop on the website, Square handles the payment on its own secure page, and Benji's Kitchen never sees your card details. Square sends us your email address and the shop ID you entered, so we can send your unlock code. We use them only for your purchase and for helping you with it.

Children

Benji's Kitchen has no accounts, no ads, and no chat, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Kids should cook with an adult watching.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described above.

Deleting your information

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To delete purchase records we hold (your email address and shop ID), email us and we'll remove them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
