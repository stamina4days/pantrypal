# Pantry Pal: testing checklist

Thanks for testing Pantry Pal! Work through the sections below on your phone. You don't have to do them all at once, and it's fine to skip anything your phone can't do.

## How to report a problem
- Use **Send feedback** in Settings or on the Help page (the ? button at the top). It opens an email with your phone model and app version already filled in.
- Say what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot.
- If the app won't open at all, email signaftersix@gmail.com and say which phone and browser you use.

## Before you start
- [ ] Open https://stamina4days.github.io/pantrypal/ in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
- [ ] Install it: Android Chrome menu > **Install app** (or Add to Home screen). iPhone Safari > Share > **Add to Home Screen**.
- [ ] The home screen icon shows Benji, the green dollar sign, not a blank square or a letter.
- [ ] Open it from the home screen. It opens full screen, without the browser address bar.
- [ ] Settings > **What works on this phone**. Take a screenshot of the list and include it with your feedback.

## First visit
- [ ] While loading, Benji stands on a crate of produce and a progress bar fills up.
- [ ] Benji's shop front appears with a paper notice: **Read the terms** first, then tick the box. The "Let's get started" sign stays locked until you've done both.
- [ ] Benji asks your name. Type it, or tap **Skip for now**.
- [ ] The start menu shows Start and four hanging signs: Benji's shop, Benji's hats, Play a game, and Take the tour. Each one opens the right place.
- [ ] Setup is two quick steps: how the app looks, then tapping the foods you have right now. The foods you tapped show up on My Food with dates, then the tour walks you through Today.
- [ ] Close the app and open it again. The tour doesn't come back on its own (it's still in Help and on the start screen).
- [ ] Delete something, like a shopping list item or a meal in your plan. An Undo button shows at the bottom. Tap it and the thing comes back.

## Music
- [ ] The first time, after you enter your name, Benji asks **Play music while you cook?** No music plays before you answer.
- [ ] Tap **Yes, play music**: music starts, and the music button at the top shows moving bars. (Or tap **No thanks**: it stays quiet, and the music button can turn it on later.)
- [ ] Close and reopen the app. Benji doesn't ask again, and music follows your answer when you tap Start.
- [ ] Tap the music button. The music menu shows what's playing, a Play or Off button, a volume slider, and every song.
- [ ] Turn music off and on with the Off and Play button.
- [ ] Move the volume slider. The music gets quieter and louder. (On an iPhone the slider may do nothing: use the phone's volume buttons instead.)
- [ ] Tap a free song. It plays, and the app remembers it next time.
- [ ] Tap **Your kitchen's song**. The music goes back to your kitchen's own song.
- [ ] Tap a locked song. A 10 second sample plays from the middle of the song, then your music comes back.
- [ ] Locked groups explain how to unlock them, and **See it in Benji's shop** opens the shop.
- [ ] Switch to another app and come back. The music pauses while you're away and starts again when you return.
- [ ] Songs need an internet connection. With airplane mode on, the rest of the app still works without music.

## Today and the tabs
- [ ] Today looks like a kitchen. Tap a fridge magnet, the fridge note, the chalkboard, the hat on the peg, the jar, the shop flyer on the fridge, and the game console: each opens the right page.
- [ ] Add food stays on screen, with no big empty gap between it and the tabs.
- [ ] The five tabs (Today, My Food, Recipes, Plan, Shopping) sit in **one row** at the bottom.
- [ ] Each tab fits on the screen without scrolling the whole page. Long lists scroll inside their own box.
- [ ] Tap anywhere on Today. Benji's eyes look toward your tap. When he says something new, his mouth moves while the words fill in.
- [ ] Tap Benji a few times. He bounces or waves his glove.
- [ ] Look out the window over the counter. The sky matches the time of day, and there's weather for the season. The clock on the freezer shows the right time.
- [ ] Turn music on. The game console's lights blink.
- [ ] Tap **All foods** on the fridge door. The door swings open, then My Food slides in.
- [ ] Mark a food as used before its date. A stamp lands, a coin flips up, and Benji catches it. Go back to Today: the food drops into your jar with a splash.
- [ ] Mark a food as thrown out. The price tag rips and falls away.
- [ ] Add food (one by hand, a receipt, or checked items on the shopping list). The food hops out of a bag and into a little fridge.
- [ ] Earn a badge. It slams down with a puff of dust.
- [ ] Open a recipe: it flips over like a card. Start cook mode: steam rises off the dish. Open Plan: the days turn like calendar pages.
- [ ] Turn on reduce motion in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings > Accessibility > Motion; Android: Settings > Accessibility > Remove animations). Nothing swings or flies; things fade in and out instead.

## Pages and moving around
- [ ] Tap a food on My Food. Its page slides in. Tap Back, and it slides away.
- [ ] Open a food page, then a recipe from it, then Cook mode. Press your phone's back button three times. You step back through each page to My Food.
- [ ] On iPhone, swipe from the left edge to go back.
- [ ] Open a recipe, copy the address from the browser, and open it in a new tab. The same recipe opens.
- [ ] Tap a shelf (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry, Counter) on My Food and a recipe card in the recipe box on Recipes.
- [ ] Search (the magnifying glass) finds a food, a recipe, and things to do like "plan my week", "music", or "feedback".

## Adding food
- [ ] On Today, the green **What can I cook?** button says how many recipes need nothing from the store. Tap it, then try each filter: Nothing to buy, Under 20 min, and Uses what's expiring.
- [ ] Run **Show Benji my kitchen** (the pantry audit). Tap about 10 items, add one in the "Something else?" box, and finish.
- [ ] My Food shows the items with dates and amounts.
- [ ] Add food with the grocery bag: type a name or tap a quick pick, pick where it goes, and pick when it goes bad.
- [ ] Swipe a food left for Used it or Tossed, and right to freeze it.
- [ ] **Say it:** tap the microphone and say "milk, a dozen eggs, and two pounds of chicken". (Works best in Chrome on Android.)

## Camera features
- [ ] **Barcode:** tap the camera button on My Food and scan a box or can. The camera opens, it beeps or vibrates, and the name fills in.
- [ ] Scan the same barcode again. It says "Found in your saved barcodes."
- [ ] **Read date:** open a food, tap Read date from package, and photograph a printed date. Check the date it filled in.
- [ ] **Receipt:** tap the receipt button and photograph a grocery receipt. Benji lists the foods he found first, with where each goes and how long it keeps, and puts lines he isn't sure about below. Fix any names that look off, then tap **Add** (it says how many foods). Tell us any receipt names he got wrong, and which store it was from.

## Planning and cooking
- [ ] Plan tab > **Plan my week** > Make my plan. Swap one meal, lock one, then Try again (the locked one stays).
- [ ] **Save and shop**, then add the items to a list.
- [ ] **Prep day:** check a few tasks, start a timer, close it, and reopen it. Your checks are still there.
- [ ] Let a timer finish with the phone **unlocked**: it beeps, vibrates, and says "Timer done."
- [ ] Let a timer finish with the **screen off**: note whether you got any alert. (This often doesn't work on phones.)
- [ ] Open a recipe > **Cook mode**. Leave the phone on the counter for 2 minutes. The screen stays on.
- [ ] Turn on **Read aloud**, then tap Next. It reads the step.
- [ ] Turn on **Voice commands**, allow the microphone, and say "next", "back", "repeat", and "timer."

## Shopping and sharing
- [ ] The shopping list is grouped by store section, with an estimated total.
- [ ] Tap the share magnet and send the list to yourself by text. Open the link on the same phone: it offers to add the list.
- [ ] Check items off, then tap **I bought these**. They move into My Food.
- [ ] Settings > **Share your kitchen** sends a link that copies your kitchen onto another phone.

## Games and hats
- [ ] Play > **Fridge Sort**: answer the cards. On a card about your real food, answer "About half" and check its amount on My Food.
- [ ] Play > **Daily food word**: guess the word, then open the recipe it unlocks.
- [ ] Win a hat (play 5 rounds of Fridge Sort, or get a perfect round) and see it on Benji's hats page.

## Benji's shop
- [ ] Settings > **Benji's shop**. Near the top, a note says your unlock code arrives by email within 24 hours.
- [ ] Every item is a poster with a price on a gold coin. The theme pack shows four mini kitchens, and each has a **Hear its song** button.
- [ ] Tap a station in the radio pack. A 10 second sample plays.
- [ ] The tip jar shows three tiers, each more golden than the last.
- [ ] Tap **Copy** next to your shop ID. Tapping Buy also copies it and opens a Square checkout page. (You don't have to buy anything.)
- [ ] **If you're testing a purchase:** paste your shop ID at checkout, pay, and wait for the email. Tap the link in it, or paste the code under **Have a code?** The item unlocks, and its music or hats show up.

## Reminders and calendar
- [ ] Settings > Reminders > turn on notifications. You get a "Reminders are on" notification.
- [ ] Settings > **Add expiry reminders to my calendar**. Open the downloaded file. Reminders show up in your calendar.
- [ ] Plan tab > **Add to calendar**. Meals show up at breakfast, lunch, and dinner times.

## Printing
- [ ] Plan tab > **Print**. The preview shows a weekly table and a shopping list.
- [ ] Prep day > **Print**. The preview shows a checklist.

## Offline
- [ ] Use the app online once, then turn on airplane mode and open it from the home screen. My Food, Recipes, Plan, and Cook mode all work.
- [ ] Turn airplane mode off again.

## Backup
- [ ] Everything you add is saved only on this phone. After you've added about 10 foods (or used the app for a week), Benji reminds you on My Food to save a copy. Tap **Save a copy** in his reminder: a file downloads and the reminder goes away.
- [ ] Settings > Keep a copy > **Save a copy** also downloads a file.
- [ ] Open the site in a different browser (or a private tab), Settings > **Bring back a copy**, and pick the file. Your food, plan, lists, and purchases come back.

## Display
- [ ] Settings > Display > **Text size**: try Large and Extra large. Everything still fits, and the tabs stay in one row.
- [ ] Turn up your phone's own text size in its settings. The app still fits and the tabs stay in one row.
- [ ] Turn on High contrast. Everything is still readable.
- [ ] Turn on your phone's dark mode. The app switches to dark colors.
- [ ] If you own the theme pack: Settings > Display > pick a kitchen theme. Every tab changes its look, and the theme's song starts playing.

## iPhone only
- [ ] With the ring/silent switch on silent, music and sounds are muted. Turn silent off to hear them.
- [ ] Music starts after you tap Start, and the music menu works.
- [ ] Swipe-back from the left edge works on every page.
- [ ] Added to the home screen, the app opens full screen and remembers your food after you close it.
