# Pantry Pal: phone testing checklist

Run this once on your own phone after you upload the site. It covers the parts that can't be tested on a computer.
Check each box, and write down anything that looks wrong (what you tapped, what happened, and your phone model).

## Before you start
- [ ] Open the site at its real https address (GitHub Pages), not as a file.
- [ ] Settings > **Check what this phone supports**. Take a screenshot of the list.
- [ ] Install it: Android Chrome menu > **Install app** (or Add to Home screen). iPhone Safari > Share > **Add to Home Screen**.
- [ ] The home screen icon shows Benji, not a blank square or a letter.
- [ ] Open it from the home screen. It opens full screen, without the browser address bar.
- [ ] First visit: Benji asks your name on the start screen, then setup, then the tour.
- [ ] Tick "Don't show this when I open the app" in the tour, reopen the app, and check the tour stays away. Turn it back on in Settings.
- [ ] Win a hat in Fridge Sort and see it on the hats page.
- [ ] Today looks like a kitchen. Tap a fridge magnet, the fridge note, the chalkboard, the hat on the peg, the jar, and the game console: each opens the right page. Add food stays on screen.
- [ ] Settings > Benji's shop shows the Fun hat pack. Tap the unlock link from a test code: the four hats unlock and Benji can wear them.
- [ ] Settings > Terms and food safety opens the terms. A food's page shows the "This date is an estimate" line, and its link opens the terms too.
- [ ] With some food added, Today shows how much food money could go to waste, and recipes say "Saves about $X".
- [ ] Plan my week shows "Rescues about $X of food" and a Freeze button for anything still at risk.
- [ ] In Fridge Sort, answer "About half" on one of your foods and check its amount on My Food.

## Opening screen and pages
- [ ] The opening screen shows Pal and a progress bar that fills as recipes load.
- [ ] Tap a food on My Food. Its page slides in. Tap Back, and it slides away.
- [ ] Open a food page, then a recipe from it, then Cook mode. Press your phone's back button three times. You should step back through each page to My Food.
- [ ] On iPhone, swipe from the left edge to go back.
- [ ] Open a recipe, copy the address from the browser, and open it in a new tab. The same recipe opens.
- [ ] Tap a shelf tile (Fridge, Freezer...) and a collection card on Recipes.

## First run
- [ ] Setup appears on first open. Try going Back and Next.
- [ ] Run the pantry audit. Tap about 10 items, add one item in the "Something else?" box, and finish.
- [ ] My Food shows the items with dates and amounts.

## Camera features
- [ ] **Barcode:** tap the camera button on My Food and scan a box or can. The camera opens, it beeps or vibrates, and the name fills in.
- [ ] Scan the same barcode again. It says "Found in your saved barcodes."
- [ ] **Read date:** open an item, tap Read date from package, and photograph a printed date. Check the date it filled in.
- [ ] **Receipt:** tap the receipt button and photograph a grocery receipt. Fix any names, uncheck non-food, and add.

## Planning and cooking
- [ ] Plan tab > **Plan my week** > Make my plan. Swap one meal, lock one, then Try again (the locked one should stay).
- [ ] **Save and shop**, then add the items to a list.
- [ ] **Prep day:** check a few tasks, start a timer, close the dialog, reopen it (checks should still be there).
- [ ] Let a timer finish with the phone **unlocked**: it should beep, vibrate, and say "Timer done."
- [ ] Let a timer finish with the **screen off**: note whether you got any alert. (This often doesn't work on phones.)
- [ ] Tap **Done: mark as prepped**. My Food shows "Prepped:" containers.
- [ ] Open a recipe > **Cook mode**. Leave the phone on the counter for 2 minutes. The screen should stay on.
- [ ] Turn on **Read aloud**, then tap Next. It reads the step.
- [ ] Turn on **Voice commands**, allow the microphone, and say "next", "back", "repeat", and "timer."

## Shopping and sharing
- [ ] Shopping list is grouped by store section, with an estimated total.
- [ ] Tap the share button and send the list to yourself by text. Open the link on the same phone: it offers to add the list.
- [ ] Check items off, then **Move checked to My Food**.

## Reminders and calendar
- [ ] Settings > Reminders > turn on notifications. You get a "Reminders are on" notification.
- [ ] Settings > **Add expiry reminders to my calendar**. Open the downloaded file. Reminders show up in your calendar.
- [ ] Plan tab > **Add to calendar**. Meals show up at breakfast, lunch, and dinner times.

## Printing
- [ ] Plan tab > **Print**. The print preview shows a weekly table and a shopping list.
- [ ] Prep day > **Print**. The preview shows a checklist.

## Offline
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode and open the app from the home screen. My Food, Recipes, Plan, and Cook mode all work.
- [ ] Turn airplane mode off again.

## Backup
- [ ] Settings > **Export backup**. A file downloads.
- [ ] Open the site in a different browser (or a private tab), Settings > **Import backup**, and pick the file. Your food, plan, and lists come back.

## Display
- [ ] Settings > Larger text and High contrast. Everything still fits on screen.
- [ ] Turn on your phone's dark mode. The app switches to dark colors.
