About Pantry Pal

Hi, I'm Benji

I'm a dollar sign, because every bit of food you save is money saved. Tell me what food you have, and I'll help you cook what's about to go bad, plan your week, and build your shopping list.

Your food stays yours

There's no account and no server. Everything you add is saved only in this browser on this phone. Export a backup in Settings to move it somewhere else.

Works offline

Once Pantry Pal loads one time, every recipe and page is saved on your phone, so it works in the store with no signal.
