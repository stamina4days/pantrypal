Terms and food safety

Last updated November 22, 2026

Check your food before you eat it

Every date in Pantry Pal is an estimate. Benji guesses how long food keeps from typical shelf life, but the real answer depends on how it was stored, how cold your fridge is, when it was opened, and the food itself.

  • Always check the date printed on the package. It comes first, before anything Pantry Pal says.
  • Look at it, smell it, and check the texture before you eat or cook anything. When in doubt, throw it out.
  • Some foods can make you sick before they look or smell bad, like meat, fish, eggs, dairy, leftovers, and cooked rice. Follow the package and food safety guidance for those.
  • A food showing "good for now" in the app is not a promise that it is safe to eat.

Allergies and diets

Diet and allergy labels on recipes are a guide and can be wrong or incomplete. Brands use different ingredients. Always read the ingredient label on every package, especially for allergies. Don't rely on Pantry Pal to keep anyone safe from an allergen.

Recipes and cooking

Cook meat, poultry, fish, and eggs all the way through, and chill leftovers promptly. Cooking times are a guide, since stoves and ovens vary. Kids should cook with an adult watching, including the steps marked as good for little helpers.

Nutrition, prices, and savings

Calories, protein, prices, and money saved are estimates based on typical values. They are not exact and may not match your store, your brands, or your portions.

Your information

There's no account. Your food, plans, lists, and settings are saved only in this browser on this device, and Pantry Pal doesn't collect them. Clearing your browser data can erase them, so export a backup in Settings to keep a copy.

A few features use outside services. Scanning a barcode sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to look up the name. Reading a date or a receipt downloads a free text reading tool the first time, and the photo is read on your phone. Fonts load from Google Fonts. A kitchen link you share contains your kitchen data, so share it only with people you trust.

If you buy something from Benji's shop, Square sends us your email address and the shop ID you entered, so we can send your unlock code. We use them only for your purchase and for helping you with it.
